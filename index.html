Sign a document

Write the document here. The app prints it with QR codes that carry your digital signature and the exact text you signed, so the Recorder can check it straight from the paper. The printed document still has the usual ink signature lines and notary block; the digital signature is an extra layer on top.

Capital letters, spacing and line breaks don't matter. Every word, number and punctuation mark does.
