# State seals: sources and credits

The state seals built into the tool (shown when a county in another state is chosen) come from
[Wikimedia Commons, Category:SVG state seals of the United States](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:SVG_state_seals_of_the_United_States),
rendered by Wikimedia as PNGs and resized to 256 pixels. Most are in the public domain; one (Maryland) is CC BY-SA 4.0.
**Insignia:** many states restrict the use of their seal by law. Their use here is subject to the "proposed program, not an official form" notice; get the state’s permission before any official use.
